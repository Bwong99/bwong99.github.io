Privacy Policy

CPEN 321 App  ·  Effective 20 September 2026

CPEN 321 App is a student coursework project for CPEN 321 Software Engineering at the University of British Columbia. This policy describes exactly what the app does with your information.

What the app accesses

When you sign in, the app requests only basic Google profile information: the openid, email and profile scopes. It does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar, photos, location, or any other Google service or device data.

How that information is used

Google returns an identity token to the app on your device. The app sends that token to its own back-end server, which verifies it with Google and replies with the first and last name it contains. The app displays that name on screen, alongside the other information the assignment requires. That is the only use.

What is stored and transmitted

  • The app does not store your name, email address, identity token, or any other personal information. Nothing is written to disk and nothing persists after you close the screen.
  • The identity token is sent to the project's back-end server, which verifies it against Google's public signing keys to confirm the sign-in is genuine. The server checks the token in memory, replies with the verified first and last name, and discards it when the request finishes. It is never written to disk and is not passed to any other party.
  • The project operates no database, no user accounts, and no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.

The back-end server

The app contacts a back-end server operated by the student author to verify your sign-in and to retrieve the server's own IP address, the server's local time, the author's name, and a relayed stream of pixel-art updates. Each of those requests carries your Google identity token so the server can confirm who is asking; the server keeps nothing from it. Its diagnostic logs record only the technical reason a sign-in was rejected, never your name, email address, or token.

Sharing

No information is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone. There is nothing collected to share.

Children

This app is coursework and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
